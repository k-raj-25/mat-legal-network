Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect information on MAT Legal Network.

Last updated: 20 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MAT Legal Network (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles personal information when you visit matlegal.in, search the lawyer directory, create a lawyer account, or contact us. By using the platform, you agree to this policy and our Terms of Use.

Who we are

MAT Legal Network is a lawyer discovery platform based in New Delhi, India. We help people find verified advocates by practice area, experience, and location. We are a data fiduciary under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 for personal data we collect through this website.

MAT Legal Network, 4th Floor, Legal Hub, Connaught Place, New Delhi 110001.
Email: privacy@matlegal.in

Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the directory, review lawyer applications, keep accounts secure, and respond to you. We do not sell personal data.

Visitors and clients

You can browse and search without creating an account. We may process:

  • Search filters you submit, such as practice area, state, city, and experience range.
  • Messages you send by email or phone, including your name and contact details.
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, device, pages viewed, and referring URL, which may appear in server logs used for security and reliability.

The on-site contact form is not active yet. If you email hello@matlegal.in, we use that correspondence only to respond to your enquiry.

Lawyers who register

When you register or reapply, we collect:

  • Account details: email address and a hashed password (we do not store your password in plain text).
  • Identity and contact: full name, office or landline number, and mobile number.
  • Professional details: Bar Council registration number, years of experience, practice areas, languages, office address, and biography.
  • Locations where you practise (state and city).
  • A profile photograph you upload.
  • Application status, including any rejection reason recorded during review.

Your Bar Council registration number is used to verify your application and to prevent duplicate listings. It is not shown on your public profile.

How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Operate search, lawyer profiles, and account login.
  • Review, approve, or reject lawyer applications and communicate about status.
  • Publish approved lawyer profiles in the public directory.
  • Respond to support, partnership, and onboarding questions.
  • Protect the platform against abuse, fraud, and unauthorised access.
  • Improve the site and meet legal or regulatory obligations.

What appears in the public directory

A lawyer profile is not public until an administrator approves it. After approval, visitors may see your name, photograph, biography, practice areas, languages, locations, years of experience, and the contact numbers you provided so that prospective clients can reach you.

Your login email and password are not published. You can review the public view of an approved profile from your account area after login.

Cookies and similar technology

We use essential cookies and session data so the site works, including keeping you signed in and protecting forms against cross-site request forgery. These cookies are required for lawyer login and registration. We do not currently use advertising or analytics cookies.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google may process your IP address and related technical data according to Google’s privacy policy.

How we share information

We may share information:

  • Publicly, as described above, for approved lawyer profiles.
  • With service providers who host the website, database, or file storage, only as needed to operate the platform.
  • If required by law, regulation, court order, or to protect the rights, safety, or security of users or the public.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or similar organisational change, subject to this policy or equivalent protections.

We do not sell personal information or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Retention

We keep account and profile data for as long as your lawyer account remains active, and for a reasonable period afterwards if needed for security, dispute handling, or legal compliance. Application records (including rejection reasons) are kept so we can administer reapplications. Server logs are kept only as long as useful for operations and security. You may ask us to delete your account as described below.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including hashed passwords, access controls, and encrypted connections where the site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please use a strong, unique password and contact us promptly if you believe your account has been compromised.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may:

  • Request access to personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request erasure of your personal data, subject to legal retention needs.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raise a grievance about how we handle your data.

Lawyers can review their submitted profile details after login. To update an approved listing, correct an error, or close an account, email privacy@matlegal.in. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

Children

MAT Legal Network is intended for adults seeking legal professionals and for practising advocates. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of the platform after an update means you accept the revised policy.
